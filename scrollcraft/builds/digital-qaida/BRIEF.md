## 2. The Scroll Journey (Beat by Beat)
1. **Recognition (Sanctuary & Invocation):** Opening with `بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَنِ الرَّحِيمِ` inside a dimensional layered architectural hero where a modern Arabic Smartboard lectern sits between sunlit mashrabiya depth planes.
2. **Tension & Mechanism (The Articulation of Sound):** Why Quranic pronunciation feels hard without guidance. The frame holds (`pin`) while the 29 Noorani Qaida letters (`পাঠ ১ / ৩০`) and their exact Makhraj (articulation anatomy, Bengali phonetic guide, and 3-step pronunciation rules) respond to touch and voice.
3. **Breadth (Six Pillars of Traditional Learning with Modern Tech):** Vertical scroll converts into lateral travel (`pan`) across the six core capabilities: Step-by-step lessons, Arabic writing board, Audio pronunciation, Quizzes & tests, Classroom system, and Progress tracking.
4. **The Peak (The Living Qalam Smartboard):** After a quiet calligraphic breath ("অল্প অনুশীলন। সুন্দর শুরু।"), the screen opens via an iris/wipe reveal into the live interactive **Arabic Writing Board Pro**. Visitors trace `بِسْمِ`, `قَلَمٌ`, `كِتَابٌ` or any of the 29 letters from right to left with a 45° calligraphy nib and replay their strokes in real time.
5. **Substance (Two Portals, One Classroom):** Interactive Split Stage comparing Ustad Abdullah's **Teacher Portal Pro** (batches `Q7KM`, `R3NP`, `X5BT`, 45 students, homework & progress) with Ahmad's **Student Portal** (Lesson 4 at 70%, class code join, daily tasks, 7-day streak).
6. **Commitment (Plans, Access & Resolution):** Transparent 60-day learning plans (`Free ০৳`, `Student Pro ৫০০৳`, `Teacher Pro ১,০০০৳`), coupon preview verifier, the 6 access rules, and a resolved sanctuary closing stage that holds on the three entry paths (`শিক্ষক পোর্টাল Pro`, `ছাত্র পোর্টাল`, `লগইন ছাড়া শিখুন →`).
